Privacy

This site keeps very little about you. Signing in uses your SNOMED International account, so most of what is known about you is held there, not here. This page lists everything this site does keep, why, and for how long.

Your SNOMED International account

You sign in with your SNOMED International account. Your account, and everything SNOMED International keeps about it, is covered by SNOMED International's privacy policy. When you sign in, your account tells this site your name, a unique account number and, if it has been verified, your email address. Your password never reaches this site.

What this site keeps

What Why How long
Your account number To know which value sets and profile are yours. It is never shown to anyone. As long as you have value sets or a profile here.
Your name Saved with each value set you make, and shown to signed-in people as who made it. Until you delete the value set.
Your email address Only if you let people message you, in your profile settings, so their messages can reach you. It is never shown to anyone. Until you switch messages off, when it is deleted.
Your organisation and picture Only if you add them in your profile settings. Shown to signed-in people beside your value sets. Until you remove them.
Your value sets What you make here, with their versions and notes. Until you delete them.
A record of messages sent Who sent a message about which value set, and when, so the number of messages can be limited. One day. The message itself is never kept.

Visitors who have not signed in are not told who made a value set, and see no names, organisations or pictures.

Messages to value set owners

When you message the owner of a value set, it is sent by email through Resend, an email delivery service that sends it on this site's behalf. Your name and email address go with the message, so the owner can reply to you. The owner's email address is not shown to you unless they reply.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in. It lasts until you sign out or close your browser, or until you have been inactive for a while.
  • A second cookie protects the site from requests made in your name by other sites.
  • Your browser also remembers whether you closed the tip on how a value set is made, and, if you are not signed in, when you can next search for concepts. Neither says anything about who you are.

There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and nothing tracks you across sites. Fonts and images are served from this site, so loading a page does not contact anyone else.

Addresses and logs

When you search for concepts without signing in, your internet address is held in memory for up to three minutes to limit how often searches can be made. Like most websites, the web server may also record your internet address and the pages you asked for, to keep the site secure and to fix problems.

Your choices and rights

You can change or remove your organisation, picture and messages setting in your profile settings, and delete any value set you made. You can also ask to see, correct or delete the personal data this site keeps about you.
